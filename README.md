# Puffer Party

<a href="https://pufferparty.org/"><img src="https://pufferparty.org/web-app-manifest-512x512.png" alt="Puffer Party pufferfish" width="96" height="96"></a>

**Pass the puffer with friends.**

Puffer Party is a free multiplayer category game. Name an unused answer, pass the puffer and keep your hearts. The last player still floating wins.

### [Play Puffer Party online →](https://pufferparty.org/)

Play in your browser with a guest nickname. No sign-up, download or daily play limit.

## How to play

1. Join a public table or create a private room for friends.
2. When the puffer reaches you, answer the current category with something nobody has used yet.
3. A valid answer passes the puffer. If it pops while you hold it, you lose a heart.
4. Keep at least one heart to stay in the game, then share your result or play another round.

[Read the rules](https://pufferparty.org/how-to-play/) for timers, hearts and room settings.

## Choose your table

- **Quick casual play:** join a public table and start a round.
- **Play with friends:** create a private room for **2–16 players** and share its invitation code or link.
- **Practice:** try Warm-up Reef, Daily Mix or Deep Dive with clearly labelled BOTs. BOT games do not affect Elo or rated wins.
